Pay per click is a product promotion strategy that only requires the advertisers pay a certain fee each time their advert posted online is clicked. This method, therefore, differs from the other methods that require users to pay a fixed amount of money for a given period or given the time of the day as media houses do it. Pay per click is a service common in search engine advertisement employed nowadays by most search engines. Search engines such as Google, Yahoo, and Bing offer these services to clients at rates that vary from one site to another.

The method is a relatively cheaper way among those used in Search engine marketing. Taking a quick example, in case you run a commercial website that deals with the sale of goods and you enter into a marketing agreement with Google. Your agreement may require you to pay up to $ 2 for every click on your advert that leads a client to your product. This can be beneficial since if single click results in the sales of goods up to $400 then your expenditure on the advert to sell this product may be only $5. The lead advert can result in a client seeing more products that may not have been advertised or even attracting him to back again for more goods.
