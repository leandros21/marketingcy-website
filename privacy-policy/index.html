Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

MarketingCy respects your privacy. This notice explains how we handle personal information when you use our website or contact us.

1. Data controller and contact

MarketingCy, Propyleon 30, Paphos, Cyprus, is the contact for privacy matters relating to this website. You can contact us at info@marketingcy.com.

2. Information we collect

When you send an enquiry or request a consultation, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, service interests, and message. Our website may also process technical data such as browser information, IP-derived security information, and usage information through analytics technologies where enabled.

3. Why we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and communicate about requested services.
  • To operate, maintain and secure our website and prevent misuse.
  • To understand website performance and improve our services, where lawful.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal bases

Where GDPR applies, processing may be based on taking steps at your request before a contract, our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries and protecting the website, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where required for non-essential analytics or cookies.

5. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager, depending on the tracking configuration in use. These technologies may collect information about site usage or use cookies. Where consent is legally required for non-essential technologies, they should not operate before valid consent is obtained. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; browser controls may affect website functionality.

6. Service providers and transfers

We may use hosting, database, email-delivery and analytics providers to operate the website and process enquiries. Depending on the provider and service, personal information may be processed outside Cyprus or the European Economic Area. Where required, appropriate legal safeguards should apply.

7. Retention

We retain personal information for no longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, taking into account follow-up needs, security, and legal obligations. Different categories of records may have different retention periods. You may contact us for information about the retention applicable to your data.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis. Contact info@marketingcy.com to make a request. You may also complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus.

9. Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No online transmission or storage system is guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Third-party links and children

Our website may link to third-party websites governed by their own privacy notices. Our services are not directed at children.

11. Updates

We may update this notice to reflect changes to our website, services or legal requirements. The current version will appear on this page.
